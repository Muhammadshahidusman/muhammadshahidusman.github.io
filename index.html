Kinara: Ravi Floodplain Watch

After the August 2025 Ravi flood, the highest since 1988, Kinara counts the estimated buildings inside the Ravi floodplain and when each first appeared, then scans every new Sentinel-1 radar pass for candidate new construction, with Sentinel-2 before/after evidence for field officers. Designed for PDMA Punjab and the Lahore district administration, in English and Urdu.

  • 216Kestimated buildings in the floodplain (243 km²)
  • 38.9Kfirst seen after 2016
  • 1.86Mpeople in the floodplain (WorldPop 2025)

The first run raised 510 candidate alerts, now awaiting field verification. The 2025 flooded-building count is held back until the flood thresholds are calibrated against field points. Basemap © OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles, OpenStreetMap contributors.

ParcelWatch: Finding Unpermitted Construction

A 4-band Mask R-CNN fine-tuned on Overture footprints finds every building on 2018 and 2022 NAIP imagery, matches them as objects and cross-checks 5,667 Wake County parcels against permits. New buildings with no permit are flagged: the service tax assessors buy from imagery vendors.

  • 325parcels with a new building, no permit
  • 59%precision, blind check (10 of 17)
  • $731Kest. missed tax per year

Labels come from Claude's review of image chips, not a field check. The public map aggregates to H3 hexagons, so no parcel, owner or address is published.

  • PyTorch
  • Mask R-CNN
  • NAIP
  • GeoPandas
  • H3

Land-Cover Classification: EuroSAT to Lahore

A custom CNN and an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet50 trained on 27,000 EuroSAT Sentinel-2 images, explained with Grad-CAM and run across 4,289 tiles of Lahore district. Both models run live in the browser.

  • 97.41%ResNet50 test accuracy
  • +16.0 ptsover the custom CNN
  • 25.7×compute cost (GMACs)

On Lahore the two models agree on only 39% of tiles, a clear sign of domain shift from European training data.

  • PyTorch
  • ResNet50
  • Grad-CAM
  • ONNX Runtime Web
